# Item category

Most items are classified into Class/ Elemental/ Wearing/ and modifier.

## Class item:

Items that only appear in the shop if you play that specific class.
Class = Ranger/ Reaper/ Berserker/ Pyromancer/ etc…
If you played Ranger, you’ll found Ranger item in the shop e.g. Lucky clover, Carrot, Shortbow, etc…

## Elemental item: Items are categorized by elemental type

e.g. Nature/ Magic/ Holy/ Dark/ Vampiric/ etc…

## Wearing item: Item that is categorized by wearing

e.g. Weapon/ Shield/ Armor/ Shoes/ Shield/ Accessory/ Food/ Pet/ Potion.

## Modifier item: Item that consumes or generates modifiers

Blocked = Absorb 1 damage per stack. Blocked was gone when it was absorbed.
Empower = Weapon damage is increased by 1 per stack.
Mana = Resource used by Magic item.
Regen = Regenerate 1 health per stack every 2 sec.
Poison = Deals 1 damage per stack every 2 seconds.
Luck = Increase accuracy +5% per stack.
Blind = Decreases accuracy by 5% per stack
Spike = Deal 1 damage back to attacker whenever you were hit by melee.
Vampiric = Heal 1 health per stack whenever you hit an enemy by melee.
Heat = All items trigger 2% faster per stack.
Cold = All items trigger 2% slower per stack.
