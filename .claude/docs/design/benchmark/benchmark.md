## Team comp Benchmark

This bring each team comp test against all test enemy team.

1. Stage 1-5:

- Make 6 team comp (3 heroes per team) test against each stage. Each team feel great.

2. Stage 6-10:

- After implementing stage 6-10, I notice that just adding 1 hero to the team (no matter if the synergy work or not), it push power level of the team a lot. A lot mean it make your team pass the current stage that you can't with current team.
- That mean the power level right now is too low. So just by adding 1 hero make a lot of impact to the team.
- Why is low power level bad?
  If 1 random hero allow player to pass the stage, then the synergy just isn't fun because synergy not matter when you can win with any random hero.

## Takeaway

1. Why is making benchmark test good hard?

We don't know have the reference point to tell which is the correct power level in the game.

2. Test each team comp against the test enemy is the most consistent test **right now**. Once we get more result, we could change the test after that.

3. Current powerlevel is low. Which mean the synergy don't matter to the player.

Power level is low to the point that adding 1 _random hero_ to the team could make the player win a lot, which mean the synergy is not matter when player can win by looking at which hero they add.
