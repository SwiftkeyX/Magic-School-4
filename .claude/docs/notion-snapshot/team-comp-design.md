What I do to create 1 team comp
</h1>

<b>This is what I do to design 1 team comp:</b>

- <b>Create identity to each team.</b>
  - <b>Each team should have different mark layout.</b> → Mark layout (mark-layout.md)
  - <b>Each team should feel distinct in combat.</b> → How to make team feel distinct? (team-feel-distinct.md)
  - <b>Each hero felt distinct.</b> → How to make hero feel distinct? (hero-feel-distinct.md)
- <b>Actual balancing.</b>
  - <b>Each team should have relative power near other team comp.</b>
  - <b>I try to make each team pass each stage milestone, making "prog"</b>
