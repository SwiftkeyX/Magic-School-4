# 📊 Benchmark

Benchmark are test to balance hero. Divided into 2 big test:

1. Hero Benchmark which test 1 hero at a time.
2. A team comp benchmark which test 1 team comp against all set stage.

---

<h1 color="blue">
Hero Benchmark
</h1>

This test each hero individually. Using 3 test:

- single DPS
- AoE DPS
- Tankiness

---

<h1 color="blue">
Team comp Benchmark
</h1>

This bring each team comp test against all test enemy team.

---

<h1 color="blue">
Takeaway
</h1>

<h4>
In Hero benchmark, I only ever look at AoE test only.
</h4>

I never look at single DPS or Tankiness ever because I think those test aren’t accurate which is fair. I think the benchmark now are not reliable, it would be once we have enough iteration.

#### Why is making benchmark test good hard?

We don't know have the reference point to tell which is the correct power level in the game.

#### Test each team comp against the test enemy is the most consistent test <b>right now</b>. Once we get more result, we could change the test after that.

#### Current powerlevel is low. Which mean the synergy don't matter to the player.

Power level is low to the point that adding 1 <i>random hero</i> to the team could make the player win a lot, which mean the synergy is not matter when player can win by looking at which hero they add.
