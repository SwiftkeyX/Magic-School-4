# How to make team feel distinct?

(Sub-page of Team comp design, under "Create identity to each team" → "Each team should feel distinct in combat".)

## Team Design rules

1. No two heroes in a team have the same shape.
2. A team mixes at least two spacing (e.g. touching + 2 away).
3. Active marks per team: {4, 3, 3, 2, 2, 2} or {4, 4, 3, 2, 2, 2}.

## Different play style

e.g. Elf is front to back, Skeleton is assassin, etc...
