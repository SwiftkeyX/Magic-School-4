# MagicSchool.Player

What the player does with the mouse and keys on the board: drag and drop heroes and items (`Dragging`), inspect and
switch target (`PlayerController`), show marks and routes (`AlignHighlight`), paint the deploy zone in the Stage Editor
(`ZonePainting`), the team-size limit (`PlayerTeamSize`).

References **Contracts, Combat, Core, Items, Input**. Referenced by nothing. Keys are read only through `Input`.
