# MagicSchool.Engine

A leaf of small engine helpers: `CurveMotion` (move along an `AnimationCurve`), `DebugTool`, `SceneHelper`.

References nothing. Referenced by **Skills, Combat, Core**. `DebugTool` and `SceneHelper` are barely used next to direct
`Debug.Log` / `Instantiate` calls; they do not decouple anything.
